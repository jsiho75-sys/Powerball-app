Strategy Information

Random: Equal-weight Quick Pick baseline.

Balanced Odd / Even: Attempts to create a balanced odd/even combination.

Hot Numbers: Favors historically frequent numbers.

Cold Numbers: Favors historically less frequent numbers.

Hot + Cold Mix: Combines historically hot and cold numbers.

Good Spread: Attempts to distribute numbers across the 1–69 range.

Weighted Frequency: Uses historical frequency as the primary weighting factor.

Weighted + Balanced: Combines frequency weighting with odd/even, low/high and sum controls.

Low + High Mix: Mixes numbers from 1–34 and 35–69.

Recency: Gives more weight to recently appearing numbers.

Hybrid AI: Combines frequency and recency.

Overdue: Favors numbers that have not appeared recently.

Decade Spread: Attempts to distribute selections across different number ranges.

Sum Balanced: Favors combinations near the historical average sum.

Pair Boost: Gives additional weight to historically recurring number pairs.

About Powerball AI Lab
